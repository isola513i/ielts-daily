# IELTS Daily

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User approved modular static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No runtime dependencies, backend, paid APIs or paid hosting. GitHub Pages with a public repository.

## Users
A Thai speaker preparing for IELTS General, studying on an iPhone in Safari. Personal use first, with no application account.

## Product Purpose
Practise useful vocabulary through short flashcard sessions. Understand a word's part of speech, simple English definition, Thai meaning and a contextual example. Hear words and examples in a chosen English accent when the device supports it.

## Operating Context
150 original beginner/intermediate words and phrases across five topics. Ten unique cards per session; retry missed cards up to three total presentations. Progress and unfinished sessions remain in the current browser only.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Reveal the answer before choosing remembered or needs practice.
- Persist American, British or Australian accent preference; device voices are free but availability and quality vary.
- Never silently substitute an accent.
- Use GitHub Pages under a repository path with HTTPS.
- No vocabulary editing, cross-device sync, daily scheduling or full offline mode in v1.
- Website content and source are public; personal progress is not uploaded.

## Brand Commitments
Working name: IELTS Daily. Thai interface; English study material. Light surfaces, dark legible text, teal primary actions. Large mobile touch targets. Code-first implementation approved.

## Evidence on Hand
The approved plan is in docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-20-ielts-daily.md. No supplied logo, photography or proprietary vocabulary collection. Definitions and examples are written for this project. Vocabulary completion does not estimate an IELTS band score.

## Product Principles
- One word sense per card.
- Make the next study action obvious.
- Keep sessions finite and resumable.
- Be honest about voice availability and saved progress.
