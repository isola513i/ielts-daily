# Vocabulary practice surface

Mode: Operate. Mobile-first, primarily iPhone Safari. Success is a finished short study round with vocabulary understood and audio preference respected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A bilingual pocket phrasebook, opened to one useful word at a time. Study is the product; the home screen is a chapter index, not a metrics dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Warm pale paper, deep blue-black ink and restrained teal. DM Sans with Noto Sans Thai, crisp 1.75px SVG icons, ruled chapter rows, compact page references, generous entry margins. Real text does the visual work.

STORY: Choose a topic, recall the word, hear it, uncover the meaning, then decide whether to practise again. Settings changes one clear accent preference.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile has a compact wordmark, a short study invitation, one wide teal start control, then a five-row chapter index. Desktop adds a quiet navigation rail and a narrow reading-notes column. The study view centres one large English entry with sound control; the definition unfolds beneath it and answer controls sit within thumb reach. Signature interaction: reveal expands the bilingual entry with a brief line-and-content transition, no 3D flip.

FORM: Grounded candidate 7, pocket phrasebook (seed ee13026e). Other grounded systems considered: annotated textbook, language-lab console, departure board, library index, newspaper reading column, exam worksheet. The approved light/teal, code-first plan remains binding. Challenger lessons retained: timetable alignment, Metro direct actions, manual section continuity, cassette audio feedback, catalog visited states, specimen word hierarchy; their competing surface costumes are declined because they obscure mobile vocabulary recall.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
